# SEO and messaging architecture — TopStitch Campaign 01

**Important:** these are *hypothesized* keywords and buyer-intent clusters, not Search Console exports or verified keyword-volume/ranking data. Validate with first-party search data before prioritizing content production.

## Searcher identity
The commercial searcher is a **repair business owner seeking marketing help**, not a person seeking furniture repair. Search-engine titles, copy, headings and internal links must preserve this distinction.

## Core page
- **Target topic:** marketing and SEO content for mobile furniture repair businesses
- **Title:** `Marketing for Mobile Furniture Repair Businesses | TopStitch Digital`
- **Meta description:** `Marketing and SEO content for mobile furniture, leather, vinyl and upholstery repair businesses. Turn real repair work into service pages, local proof and helpful content. Request a visibility review from TopStitch Digital.`
- **H1:** `You fix the furniture. We help people find you.`
- **H2s:** Visibility gap / Different buyers, different questions / How we turn real jobs into content / Example / Fit / Visibility review / FAQs
- **CTA:** `Get a free visibility review`
- **Proof rule:** illustrative examples explicitly labeled; real clients/results only with permission.

## Written positioning framework
- **Context:** You are skilled at fixing upholstery and leather, but customers cannot evaluate unseen capabilities.
- **Evidence:** Show the kind of real jobs, repair assessments, geographic coverage, material limitations and service distinctions the operator can substantiate.
- **Professional question:** "Does your website explain the work you want more of clearly enough for a local homeowner or facilities buyer to recognize it?"
- **Offer:** A specific visibility review, then a scoped ongoing content system if fit is proven.

## Buyer-language split
**Residential:** cracked leather sofa, pet scratches, seam repairs, faded vinyl, repair versus replacement, on-site visit, service area. These are illustrative *end-user intents* used to demonstrate content that TopStitch could build for the client; they are not TopStitch's own SEO targets.

**Commercial:** restaurant booth upholstery, hotel furniture maintenance, dental/medical seating, office chair wear, fleet seating, scheduled on-site repairs. The commercial page should include operational constraints and project evidence when verified; never imply these services are provided by TopStitch.

## SEO implementation
- One canonical URL on final production deployment, no canonical on staging until destination approved.
- Meaningful heading hierarchy, semantic HTML, descriptive anchors, sitemap/robots only after domain is confirmed.
- Organization/Service schema only with verified brand name, URLs, business descriptions and contact details. Do not add fake `AggregateRating`, `Review` or `LocalBusiness` data.
- Image alt descriptions should state content, never keyword-stuff.
- Search Console tracking after launch: brand/nonbrand queries, impressions, CTR, page indexing, qualified form leads by landing page.

## Reporting loop
Monthly: source queries/landing pages, traffic by qualified channel, content shipped/updated, clicked review CTAs, accepted review forms, qualified leads, booked consults. Never use unverified traffic/ranking claims in public copy.
